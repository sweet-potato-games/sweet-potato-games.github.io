Legions Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

This policy explains what information the Android game Legions (the "Game"), made by Sweet Potato Games ("we", "us"), collects and how it is used.

In short: the Game has no accounts and no in-app purchases, and we never ask for your name, email or contacts. It uses Google services to show ads, report crashes and count gameplay events. Those services collect some device information, described below. We don't sell your information.

Information collected by the Game

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game shows ads from Google AdMob: banners on some screens and a full-screen ad when a game starts. To show and measure ads, Google may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID
  • device information, such as model, operating system version, language and screen size
  • your IP address, which can indicate your approximate location
  • how you interact with ads, such as views and taps

Players in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent through Google's consent message before personalized ads are shown. You can change that choice at any time from Settings > Privacy Options in the Game. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your Android device settings.

Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

If the Game crashes, a crash report is sent so we can fix the problem. It contains technical details about the crash and your device (such as device model, operating system version and the state of the Game when it crashed) and a random identifier for the installation. It doesn't contain your name or contact details.

Gameplay statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

The Game records gameplay events, such as which faction was picked, when a game starts, ends or is abandoned, units bought, battle armies and results, and which screens are opened, so we can balance and improve the Game. These events are linked to a random identifier for the app installation, along with basic device information and approximate location derived from your IP address. We have turned off advertising ID collection and ad personalization for these statistics, and we use them for game design only.

Your saved game

Your saved game and settings are stored only on your device. They're never uploaded to us.

How the information is used

  • to show ads, which keep the Game free
  • to find and fix crashes
  • to understand how the Game is played, so we can balance and improve it

We don't sell your information or share it with anyone other than the Google services named above.

Third-party services

The information above is collected and processed by Google under its own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services
  • Privacy and security in Firebase

Google keeps this information for the periods described in those policies and in our Firebase and AdMob settings.

Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the Game, contact us and we will help have it deleted.

Your choices and rights

  • Ad consent: change it any time from Settings > Privacy Options in the Game (where available in your region).
  • Advertising ID: reset or delete it, or opt out of personalized ads, in your Android device settings.
  • Stop all collection: uninstalling the Game stops all collection.
  • Access or deletion: depending on where you live, you may have the right to request access to or deletion of your information. Contact us and we'll help. Because we don't collect names or emails, we may need the details of your device to find the data.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the "Last updated" date above.
